How to interpret the evidence

The Trello export contains 85 cards grouped into 13 lists from Business Planning through Launch. It includes task descriptions and user stories. The portfolio uses those list names and task titles as evidence, and clearly separates the requested completion assumption from facts directly shown by the export. Proposed team responsibilities are role descriptions, not a claim that separate employees were appointed.
