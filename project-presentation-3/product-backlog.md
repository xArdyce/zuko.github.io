## Purpose

The product backlog is the ordered inventory of work needed to create, operate, and improve Nexus Setups. The task titles below come from the supplied Trello export, grouped by the 13 lists on the board. Priority levels are an assessment-level recommendation; they were not supplied as numerical scores in the export.

**Priority guide:** **P1** = foundational or required for core operation; **P2** = client delivery and launch validation; **P3** = improvement and optimisation. **Outstanding** = legal/compliance action not assumed complete.

## Refinement and prioritisation

- Keep the three legal/compliance cards outstanding until evidence of completion is available.
- Add measurable acceptance criteria to features such as registration, project submission, file uploads, billing, and revision requests.
- Record dependencies between account access, client portal, project management, and delivery workflows.
- Estimate effort and assign an owner before selecting work for a sprint.
- Separate core launch requirements from later enhancements if capacity becomes constrained.

## Reflection

The board covers the full service lifecycle, from business planning and branding to client-facing systems, production, payments, marketing, and launch. Grouping the backlog makes dependencies easier to see: for example, project submission and status tracking support the editing workflow, while authentication and role management support secure access. The priority scheme in this portfolio is a reasoned proposal rather than a recorded Trello ranking. The most important distinction is that legal and compliance tasks remain outstanding even though the assessment assumes other work is complete.
