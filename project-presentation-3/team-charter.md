# 1. Agile Team Charter

[← Back to portfolio overview](index.html)

## Project purpose

Nexus Setups is a creator-focused service concept offering video editing, thumbnail design, and SEO/tag optimisation. The project aims to make it easier for creators to receive consistent content support through clear service packages, organised project tracking, review and revision workflows, and reliable client communication.

## Vision

Establish Nexus Setups as a reliable, organised creator-support service that helps creators improve the consistency and presentation of their content.

## Objectives

- Define the business model, target audience, value proposition, pricing, and success measures.
- Build a recognisable brand and a website that explains the service.
- Provide secure account access and a client portal.
- Allow clients to submit projects, upload assets, and track progress.
- Establish editing, quality assurance, revision, delivery, and communication workflows.
- Support quotes, invoices, payment tracking, administration, and analytics.
- Test the service, document it, and prepare for launch.
- Keep legal and compliance responsibilities visible until properly completed.

## Scope

**Included in the project:** business planning; branding; website; authentication; client portal; project management; editing workflow; client communication; payments; marketing; administration; testing; documentation; and launch preparation.

**Outstanding legal/compliance work:** the Trello board contains three explicit cards that are not treated as complete:
- **Register Nexus Setups as a Business** — complete the formal business registration.
- **Register for Tax (SARS)** — complete applicable tax registration.
- **Secure Business Insurance** — obtain appropriate insurance cover.

Other non-legal work is treated as completed for this assessment, following the user's instruction. This assumption is not represented as a completion status recorded in the CSV.

## Responsibilities

The following responsibilities describe the functions needed to run the project. The Trello export does not establish that separate people were appointed to every role; in a small project, one person may cover several functions.

| Role / function | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Founder / Product Owner | Maintain the vision, decide scope, and prioritise work by value and risk. |
| Project Coordinator | Maintain the Trello board, organise planning and reviews, and keep blockers visible. |
| Website / Technical Lead | Coordinate website, authentication, portal, access, monitoring, and system settings. |
| Production Lead | Maintain editing stages, revision handling, quality checks, and delivery standards. |
| Client / Account Support | Coordinate onboarding, project communication, notifications, and revision requests. |
| Finance / Administration | Coordinate quotes, invoices, payment tracking, and administrative reporting. |
| Marketing Lead | Maintain SEO, content marketing, social campaigns, lead generation, and email marketing. |

## Working agreements

1. Keep project work visible on the board and group it by a clear delivery theme.
2. Describe work in terms of the stakeholder need and intended benefit.
3. Break broad outcomes into tasks that can be checked.
4. Make review, revision, quality assurance, and delivery explicit.
5. Protect client files and apply appropriate access and security practices.
6. Use testing and feedback to improve the service rather than assuming the first version is final.
7. Keep legal and compliance tasks marked as outstanding until the required actions and reviews are complete.

## Definition of done

A non-legal task is considered done for this assessment when its described outcome is assumed to have been produced and checked, in line with the user's instruction. In actual project management, evidence should include a working deliverable, acceptance checks, an owner, and any required handover notes. The three legal/compliance tasks listed above are not considered done.

## Evidence from Trello

The charter aligns with cards such as **Define Business Vision**, **Create Business Model Canvas**, **Design Company Logo**, **Design Homepage**, **Implement User Registration**, **Create Client Dashboard**, **Submit New Project**, **Define Editing Workflow**, **Implement Invoicing System**, **Implement User Management**, and **Conduct Beta Testing**.

## Reflection

The charter makes the project's purpose and scope easier to understand before looking at individual tasks. The board demonstrates that Nexus Setups requires more than creative production: it also needs customer access, project tracking, payments, communication, marketing, administration, and testing. A key project-management lesson is to distinguish operational readiness from legal readiness. Even if non-legal tasks are assumed complete for the assessment, formal registration, tax obligations, and insurance remain outstanding and must not be hidden by a general claim that the project is finished.
